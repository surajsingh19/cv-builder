🚀 Usage Guide
Navigating the Hub
The space is organized into strict, template-driven folders to ensure you always know where to look and what to expect:

📁 Case Studies: Deep dives into past projects, outcomes, and learnings.

📁 Best Practices: Standardized approaches to our recurring challenges.

📁 Project Execution Playbook: Step-by-step guides for running our projects.

📁 Tools & Technology Guide: Documentation on the software and systems we use.

📝 Blogs: An open-forum space. Anyone can publish a blog here at any time to share thoughts, updates, or informal learnings without needing a review.

Meet Your AI Agent
You don't have to search manually. You can use our Knowledge Agent to interact with this space. The agent can:

Answer Questions: Ask it anything about our playbooks, tools, or case studies (e.g., "What is our standard process for onboarding a new tool?").

Write & Edit: Ask the agent to draft a page for you based on your notes, or edit an existing page to match our templates.

Guide Contributions: Help you submit new official documentation (see below).

✍️ Contribution & Governance Pipeline
We encourage everyone to contribute to the Hub! To prevent the space from becoming messy, all new content (except Blogs) goes through our automated pipeline. You don't need to worry about formatting or putting it in the right place—our AI handles the heavy lifting.

How to Contribute:

Create a Draft: Go to the /Contribution folder and create a new page with your rough content, notes, or ideas.

Chat with the Agent: Our AI Agent will automatically greet you and ask which section this content belongs to (e.g., Case Studies or Best Practices).

Gap Analysis: The Agent will silently compare your draft against the official template for that section. If any key information is missing, it will ask you a few quick follow-up questions to fill in the blanks.

Automated Formatting: Once everything is captured, the Agent will rewrite and format your content perfectly into the standard template.

Review & Publish: The Agent moves the formatted page into the correct folder as a hidden draft and automatically tags the Section Owner. The owner reviews the page, ensures it meets our quality standards, and clicks "Publish" to make it visible to the whole team.

Bottom line: Just bring your knowledge to the /Contribution folder. The Agent and the Section Owners will take care of the rest.
